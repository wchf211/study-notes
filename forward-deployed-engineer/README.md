## What the course is

The FDE is an embedded engineer who closes the gap between what a model *can* do and a system that works reliably in production inside a customer organization. The course covers the full operating model — discovery → scoping → POC → pilot → handoff → field feedback — plus AI systems architecture (LLM-based vs. agentic, harness, orchestration, framework choice), FDE interview fundamentals, three full case studies (RAG, multi-agent, fine-tuning), and interview guides for Palantir, OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google Cloud.
